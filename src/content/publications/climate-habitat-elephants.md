Modelled present and future habitat suitability for Asian elephants across Bhutan using an ensemble of four algorithms calibrated with 252 verified presences, 837 verified absences, and 17 environmental predictors. Identified 4,670 km² of suitable habitat — 57.1% of it outside protected areas — and 4,160 km² of climate-stable refugia under CMIP6 scenarios, of which 53.6% also lies outside the protected-area network. The results indicate that Bhutan's existing protected-area network captures less than half of the climate-resilient habitat available to elephants, with direct implications for corridor designation and range-wide adaptation planning.
