Quantified aquatic beetle (Coleoptera) diversity along an altitudinal gradient in Bhutanese waterways and assessed relationships with physicochemical water-quality parameters. Documented how elevation and stream conditions structure macroinvertebrate communities in Himalayan headwater systems, contributing baseline biodiversity evidence for freshwater conservation planning in a globally significant mountain hotspot.
