Examined wild edible and medicinal plant (WEMP) diversity, household dependence, gendered roles, and knowledge transmission in forest-adjacent communities of Bhutan through integrated village-level evidence. The study addresses a documented gap in village-scale WEMP research and connects botanical knowledge to community-linked forest stewardship and non-wood forest product management under Bhutan's NWFP governance framework.
