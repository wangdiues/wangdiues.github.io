Combined GEDI-derived above-ground biomass, ESA CCI biomass, Sentinel-2 spectral predictors, MODIS NPP, SoilGrids soil carbon, and CMIP6 bioclimatic layers to map a 2024 baseline forest carbon surface for Bhutan at 100 m resolution and assess its climate-scenario sensitivity under SSP126, SSP245, SSP370, and SSP585 through 2100 using a gradient-boosting climate-transfer model trained on ten GCMs. The mapped stock totals 395.2 Mt C (1,450.3 Mt CO₂e), with 79.5% concentrated in the 1,000–4,000 m elevation belt. Independent NFI validation (RMSE 116.7 Mg ha⁻¹) bounds interpretation: across SSP126–370 sequestration potential remains stable or slightly positive through the century, while under SSP585 it turns marginally negative late-century (−0.56 Mg ha⁻¹) — signals smaller than validation error, positioning the results as directional indicators rather than forecasts. The clearest signal is the expansion of novel-climate conditions to 20.5% of forested area under late-century SSP585, disproportionately where national carbon stock resides.
