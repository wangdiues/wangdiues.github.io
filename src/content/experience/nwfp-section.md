- Administer national-level non-wood forest product (NWFP) allocation processes, utilization permits, quotas, and royalty-related documentation to support equitable resource distribution, sustainable utilization, and regulatory compliance.
- Monitor harvesting and utilization trends and contribute to NWFP management plans, sustainable harvesting guidelines, and national forestry policy and planning processes.
- Support resource assessments and inventories of commercially and ecologically important NWFP species and provide technical guidance on species identification, data collection, sustainable harvesting, and compliance monitoring.
- Promote community-based NWFP management by supporting local groups in sustainable resource use, participatory management, institutional strengthening, and linking forest conservation with rural livelihoods and income-generation opportunities.
- Contribute to the development of a forest-based bioeconomy by supporting sustainable commercialization, value-chain development, value addition, market linkages, and responsible private-sector engagement for NWFPs.
- Prepare technical inputs for forestry planning documents, annual reports, management guidelines, policy processes, and cross-agency coordination.
- Liaise with divisional forest offices, community groups, government agencies, research institutions, and private-sector stakeholders on NWFP implementation, reporting, sustainable resource management, and field-level issues.
