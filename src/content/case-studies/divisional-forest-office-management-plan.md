Prepared the Divisional Forest Office Management Plan for Sarpang covering 2026–2036, aligning conservation priorities, sustainable resource use, and institutional responsibilities into an operative ten-year framework.

The plan integrates biodiversity corridors, climate considerations, and community-use provisions with divisional operational programming — serving as the reference document for field implementation and annual planning under the Department of Forests and Park Services.
