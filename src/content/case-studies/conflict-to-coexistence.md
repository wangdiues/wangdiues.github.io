Prepared the Conflict-to-Coexistence (C2C) strategy for Sarpang Forest Division — one of Bhutan's highest human–elephant conflict landscapes — including gewog-level strategies for Dekiling and Gakiling covering 2026–2030.

The work translated spatial conflict evidence and community realities into a policy-aligned action framework, giving field officers and communities a structured basis for mitigation investment, rapid response, and long-term coexistence planning.
