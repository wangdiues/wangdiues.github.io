  summary: "Great hornbills depend on connected forest, large nest trees and dependable fruiting habitat. In Bhutan's 407 km² Biological Corridor 03, forest degradation, road expansion and climate-driven shifts are reducing nesting trees and safe movement routes. Because local movement data remain limited, managers need stronger evidence on flight pathways, feeding areas, breeding habitat and connectivity bottlenecks. Connected Skies will combine telemetry, bioacoustics and spatial analysis to turn those gaps into practical conservation guidance."

      description: "Under approved veterinary and animal-welfare protocols, the team plans to deploy supervised, welfare-compliant solar GPS tags. Quality-controlled locations will reveal how hornbills move through the corridor."

        - Habitat use
    - title: AI Bioacoustics
      description: "AudioMoth recorders will sample monitoring sites across elevation, forest and disturbance gradients. BirdNET will screen recordings, with detections manually validated for repeated-survey analysis."
      points:
        - AudioMoth deployment
        - BirdNET AI detection
        - Occupancy monitoring
    - title: GIS and Connectivity Modelling
      description: "Telemetry, acoustic detections and environmental layers will be integrated in GIS to identify priority habitat, corridor bottlenecks and restoration opportunities for BC-03 management."
      points:
        - Habitat mapping
        - Corridor assessment
        - Threat analysis
